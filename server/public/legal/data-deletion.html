Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: February 22, 2026

PeopleWallet allows you to delete your account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to delete your data and what happens when you do.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: Delete In-App

  1. Open PeopleWallet on your device.
  2. Go to Settings (tap the gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Option 2: Email Request

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by sending an email to support@peoplewallet.app from the email address associated with your account. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line.

What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Your user profile (name, email, avatar, bio, pronouns, location)
  • Professional information (job title, company, school, major)
  • Appearance data (height, hair color, features)
  • Social media links
  • All contacts you created or imported
  • Contact notes, tags, and phone numbers
  • Messages and conversation history
  • Club memberships and follow status
  • Event RSVPs and attendance records
  • Push notification registrations
  • Notification preferences
  • School email verification records
  • LinkedIn connection data

What Data May Be Retained

In limited circumstances, we may retain certain data after account deletion:

  • Legal compliance: Data required to be retained by law or to resolve disputes.
  • Aggregate analytics: De-identified, aggregated usage data that cannot be traced back to you.

Timeline

Account deletion is processed immediately when performed in-app. For email requests, deletion will be completed within 30 days of receiving your request.

Important Notes

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • You will be signed out immediately after deletion.
  • If you wish to use PeopleWallet again, you will need to create a new account.
  • Messages you sent to other users may still appear in their conversation history.
