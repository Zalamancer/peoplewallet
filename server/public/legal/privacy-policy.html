Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 22, 2026

PeopleWallet ("we," "our," or "us") operates the PeopleWallet mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this policy carefully. By using PeopleWallet, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • School email address (for .edu verification)
  • School affiliation
  • LinkedIn profile data (if you sign in with LinkedIn): name, email, profile photo, LinkedIn ID
  • Firebase authentication UID (if applicable)

1.2 Profile Information

You may optionally provide:

  • Nickname and pronouns
  • Bio and location
  • Avatar/profile photo
  • Professional information: job title, company, department, school, major, graduation year
  • Appearance data: height range, hair color, glasses, distinguishing features
  • Profile visibility preferences

1.3 Contact Data

With your permission, we import contact information from your device, including:

  • Contact names
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Company/organization
  • Notes

You may also manually create contacts with additional fields such as tags, notes, and social media handles.

1.4 Social Media Handles

You may provide links to your profiles on third-party services, including LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X, and GitHub.

1.5 Communications

When you use our in-app messaging feature, we collect:

  • Message content (text, GIFs)
  • Message metadata (timestamps, sender/recipient, conversation membership)
  • Read receipts and delivery status

1.6 Audio & Transcription Data

If you use our AI-powered contact capture feature:

  • Voice recordings are sent to Deepgram for speech-to-text transcription. Audio recordings are processed in real-time and are not retained by us or Deepgram after transcription.
  • Transcripts are sent to Anthropic (Claude AI) for contact information extraction. Transcripts are processed and not retained after extraction.

1.7 Event & Attendance Data

  • RSVPs and event interactions (interested, going, saved)
  • Check-in records (QR code or manual)
  • Attendance history
  • Club membership and follow status

1.8 Location Data

  • Campus geocoding data (school location coordinates via Google APIs)
  • Event venue coordinates

We do not collect continuous or background location data from your device.

1.9 Device Information

  • Push notification tokens (Expo push tokens)
  • Device platform type (iOS/Android)

1.10 Usage Analytics

  • App usage events and interactions collected via Mixpanel
  • Error and crash reports collected via Sentry

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Enable contact management and relationship tracking
  • Process voice recordings into contact information using AI
  • Facilitate in-app messaging between users
  • Display event feeds and enable event attendance features
  • Provide club discovery and membership features
  • Send push notifications (event reminders, reconnect suggestions, weekly digests)
  • Verify school email addresses for student features
  • Generate networking insights and analytics
  • Suggest co-attendees and mutual connections
  • Improve and optimize the App
  • Respond to support requests

3. AI & Automated Processing

PeopleWallet uses artificial intelligence and automated processing in the following ways:

  • Speech-to-Text Transcription: Voice recordings are processed by Deepgram's AI to convert speech into text.
  • Contact Information Extraction: Transcribed text is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to extract structured contact information (names, phone numbers, emails, companies, etc.).
  • Event Analysis: Social media posts from clubs are analyzed by AI to identify and categorize campus events.
  • Connection Suggestions: We use automated processing to suggest potential connections and co-attendees based on shared event attendance.

AI-generated results are provided as suggestions and may not be perfectly accurate. You can review and edit all AI-extracted information before saving.

4. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following third-party service providers:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — Processes transcripts to extract contact information. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Deepgram — Provides speech-to-text transcription. Deepgram Privacy Policy
  • LinkedIn — OAuth authentication and profile data. LinkedIn Privacy Policy
  • Resend — Sends email verification codes. Resend Privacy Policy
  • Mixpanel — Usage analytics and event tracking. Mixpanel Privacy Policy
  • Sentry — Error monitoring and crash reporting. Sentry Privacy Policy
  • Google APIs — Campus location geocoding. Google Privacy Policy
  • Expo — Push notification delivery. Expo Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Recipients

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: Your profile information is shared according to your visibility settings (public, mutual only, or private).
  • Service providers: As described in Section 4, with third-party vendors who process data on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or legal process.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.
  • With your consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data as follows:

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account.
  • Email verification codes: Expire and are deleted after 10 minutes.
  • Appearance data: Retained for up to 12 months or until account deletion, whichever comes first.
  • Event post embeds: Cached for up to 72 hours.
  • Audio recordings: Not retained; processed in real-time and discarded.
  • Audit logs: Retained indefinitely for security and compliance purposes.
  • Messages: Retained until you delete them or your account.

When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except for data we are required to retain for legal or compliance purposes.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data via Settings > Account > Delete Account, or by emailing us.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format (data export feature coming soon).
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of push notifications via Settings > Notifications, and control profile visibility via Settings > Privacy & Data.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at support@peoplewallet.app.

9. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access, rectification, erasure, and data portability.
  • Right to restrict or object to processing.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Our legal basis for processing your data includes: your consent, performance of a contract (our Terms of Service), and our legitimate interests in operating and improving the App.

10. Children's Privacy

PeopleWallet is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@peoplewallet.app, and we will take steps to delete such information.

11. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • AES-256-GCM encryption for sensitive data at rest
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • JWT-based authentication with token expiration
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Parameterized database queries to prevent injection attacks

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

12. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States. These countries may have different data protection laws. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to these countries. We take steps to ensure your data receives adequate protection in accordance with applicable laws.
